Data Deletion

Last Updated: May 31, 2026


Transparency and user control are at the heart of Link My Way. This policy explains how you can manage your personal data and request its permanent removal from our systems.

Overview

Link My Way (LMW) respects your privacy and your right to control your personal information.

Users may request the deletion of their account and associated personal information at any time. This page explains how to submit a deletion request and how we process such requests.

Once a deletion request is verified and approved, personal information associated with the account will be removed from our active systems unless retention is required by law.

how_to_reg How to Request

To request account and data deletion:

  • 1

    Contact us by phone or email.

  • 2

    Provide your registered mobile number and account information.

  • 3

    Our support team may contact you to verify ownership of the account.

  • 4

    Once verified, we will process your deletion request.

Timeframes

Verification

Verification requests are generally reviewed within 7 business days.

Processing

Approved deletion requests are typically completed within 30 days.

Certain information may be retained when required by applicable laws, regulatory obligations, dispute resolution requirements, or fraud prevention purposes. Any retained information will be securely stored and used only for those purposes.
